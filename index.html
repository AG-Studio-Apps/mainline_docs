Mainline is a native iPhone app for indie iOS developers. Edit metadata, manage subscriptions, track builds, and trigger CI workflows — all from your iPhone.

📱 App screenshot — dashboard overview

Everything your apps need, one app away.

📝
Edit metadata on the go

Update your app name, subtitle, description, keywords, and What's New copy across every locale — with one-tap AI translation to fill sibling languages.

💳
In-app purchases & subscriptions

Create products, set pricing tiers, configure introductory offers, and manage territory overrides for every storefront — all from your iPhone.

🚀
Submissions & releases

Submit for review with a pre-flight checklist, manage phased rollouts, and push to 100% — all from your iPhone.

🔧
CI at your fingertips

Trigger GitHub Actions workflows and watch run logs in real time so you know whether your build is passing before you land.

🔐
Code signing, sorted

Renew certificates, regenerate provisioning profiles, and sync everything to your cert repo — all from the app, no Mac required for day-to-day ops.

✨
AI-assisted drafting

Generate listing copy drafts and translations with Claude or ChatGPT. Every AI suggestion passes through a human review step — nothing goes to App Store Connect without your approval.
