## Reviews section

The **Reviews** section on the App Detail view shows a summary:

- Total reviews loaded
- Number of unanswered reviews (ones you haven't replied to yet)
- Average star rating

Tap **See All Reviews** to open the full scrollable list.

---

## Reading reviews

Reviews are sorted newest first. Each row shows:

- Star rating
- Review title and body
- Reviewer name and territory
- Date
- Whether you've replied

Tap a review to read it in full.

---

## Replying to a review

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Tap a review to open it.</li>
  <li>Tap <strong>Reply</strong> (or <strong>Edit Reply</strong> if you've already replied).</li>
  <li>Write your response. There's no enforced character limit, but keep replies concise — they appear publicly on the App Store.</li>
  <li>Tap <strong>Submit Reply</strong>. The response is sent to ASC and becomes visible on the App Store after a short propagation delay.</li>
</ol>

### AI-assisted reply suggestions

If you have an AI provider connected, tap **Suggest Reply** in the reply editor to generate a draft response based on the review content. The suggestion appears in the editor for you to read, edit, and either use or discard.

## Filtering reviews

Use the filter controls at the top of the reviews list to show:

- Only unanswered reviews
- Reviews by star rating (1-star, 2-star, etc.)
- Reviews from a specific territory
